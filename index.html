Welcome to Paradise

AQI Skanes Family Resort

Skanes, Monastir Governorate, Tunisia
4.5 / 5 (Excellent)

Nestled along the pristine shores of the Mediterranean Sea in Skanes, Monastir, AQI Skanes Family Resort offers an unparalleled blend of luxury, comfort, and authentic Tunisian hospitality. Surrounded by lush tropical gardens and golden sandy beaches, this resort is the perfect destination for families, couples, and discerning travelers seeking a memorable getaway.

With easy access to Monastir's historic landmarks, vibrant souks, and the international airport just a short drive away, the resort serves as an ideal base to explore the enchanting Sahel region of Tunisia.
